We’re excited to share that CodeHS acquired Tynker in May 2025. For more than a decade, CodeHS has worked with schools and districts to expand access to high-quality computer science education. Throughout that time, we’ve admired the impact Tynker has had in helping younger learners build confidence and creativity through coding.
