# Tynker

> Tynker is the world's leading coding platform for kids and teens ages 5-18. Tynker makes it fun and easy to learn programming and develop problem-solving and critical thinking skills through game-based, interactive courses. Tynker powers the creativity of over 100 million students and serves thousands of schools and educators worldwide. Kids start with visual block-based coding and progress to real-world languages like Python and JavaScript.
